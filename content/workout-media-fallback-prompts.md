# Workout Category Fallback Prompts

These prompts produced the four category-level fallback images on 2026-07-31
using OpenAI's built-in ImageGen tool. The tool did not expose a specific
underlying model version. The outputs are decorative artwork, not exercise-form
demonstrations.

All final assets were center-cropped to 1600×900, encoded as WebP at quality 82,
and visually inspected after conversion.

## Shared direction

- **Use case:** stylized concept
- **Asset type:** AquaZeroFit workout category fallback artwork
- **Canvas:** 16:9 landscape card image
- **Backdrop:** deep ocean-black wellness atmosphere with subtle glass panels
  and faint aquatic light
- **Style:** polished editorial, vector-like raster illustration with clean
  shapes and subtle depth
- **Crop:** subject inside the middle 55% square-safe region
- **Palette:** `#0e1416`, `#161d1e`, `#2fd9f4`, `#45dfa4`, and `#859397`
- **Constraints:** no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, brand marks,
  sexualized framing, busy scenes, or specific exercise instruction

## Strength

Create a premium abstract strength-training illustration communicating
resistance and power. Show a centered, simplified, gender-neutral athletic
silhouette in a static neutral stance beside an abstract dumbbell and balanced
geometric resistance shapes. Do not put a barbell on the person and do not show
a lifting pose.

## Cardio

Create a premium abstract cardio illustration communicating rhythm, endurance,
and forward energy. Show a centered, simplified, gender-neutral athletic
silhouette in a neutral upright walking stance, surrounded by one flowing aqua
motion ribbon and restrained pulse-wave geometry. Do not show a treadmill or a
specific sport.

## Core

Create a premium abstract core-training illustration communicating stability,
balance, and centered control. Show a centered, simplified, gender-neutral
athletic silhouette in a neutral upright stance, surrounded by concentric
balance rings and a restrained center-of-mass motif around the torso. Do not
show a plank, crunch, transparent body, or internal organs.

## Mobility

Create a premium abstract mobility illustration communicating range, ease,
flexibility, and recovery. Show a centered, simplified, gender-neutral athletic
silhouette in a neutral relaxed standing pose, surrounded by soft open arcs and
flowing ribbon geometry. Do not show a lunge, yoga pose, or specific stretch.
