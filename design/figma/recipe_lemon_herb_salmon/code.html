Method

1

Prepare Glaze

Whisk together miso paste, a splash of water, and a touch of honey until smooth. Coat the salmon fillets generously and let marinate for 15 minutes.

2

Cook Quinoa

Rinse quinoa and combine with 1 cup of water in a small pot. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered for 12-15 minutes until water is absorbed and grains are tender.

3

Sear Salmon & Veg

Heat a non-stick pan over medium-high. Place salmon skin-side down for 4 minutes until crispy. Add asparagus to the pan. Flip salmon and cook for another 2-3 minutes.
