# Adopted-upstream candidates — review queue (NOTHING HERE IS APPROVED OR LIVE)

Companion to `adopted-upstream-candidates.json`. Generated by inspection of the mirrored
wger images on disk, not by name matching alone — every proposal below was opened and looked at.

**Status: `pending-form-safety-review`.** Nothing in this file is shipped. `apps/api/src/data/media/adopted-upstream-manifest.json`
was not touched, and neither was anything else under `apps/api/src/`. Entries in that manifest go live
the moment they land, because `curatedMedia.ts` reads it at import with no per-entry review flag —
so an image only moves from this queue into that manifest after a qualified human confirms it is
correct and safe as exercise-form guidance (`docs/plans/workout-media/plan.md:32`,
`docs/plans/workout-media/todo.md:49`). The candidates JSON deliberately has no `reviewedAt` field.

## Data problems found while checking

1. **Every one of the 360 attribution records has its bytes on disk** — no missing files. The only 4
   files on disk without an attribution record are the intentional category fallbacks under
   `apps/api/assets/exercises/fallbacks/`.
2. **The wger "Plank" record (`c9e57bbe-e839-44c6-861d-1c8dd2845e36`) is dirty.** Of its 9 mirrored files:
   - 5 are byte-identical duplicates of one another (`b7bd9c28`, `902e6836`, `d1ca4a79`, `b180ce8b`, `167db646`, all md5 `d214c11fadd1`);
   - 2 more are a second identical pair (`e13f92a4`, `122735cf`, md5 `fc7f8674ea03`) — `e13f92a4` is the one already live for `ex-plank`;
   - `bce9a15d` is a **silhouette of a seated V-sit / leg raise, not a plank**;
   - `2c43d623` is **not exercise media at all** — it is a 7 KB screenshot of a statistics table
     (regression output with Turkish column headers). It should never reach a user.
3. **`One Arm Bent Row` (`6b6c69bc/1987a039.jpg`) carries a third-party copyright notice** —
   "© February 2008 HOIST® Fitness Systems" is printed in the image, and it depicts a cable machine.
   The attribution record claims CC-BY-SA 4 by `anto.kreegyr`. Worth a licence check independent of this queue.
4. **`Lunges` (`3284fd27/5c7ffe68.png`) carries FitnessVolt branding** (logo, social handles and a
   watermark) baked into the artwork, again under a CC-BY-SA 4 record. Avoided in favour of the
   Everkinetic drawing for that reason.
5. Several records are named for a movement they do not depict — `Bent Over Dumbbell Rows` is a
   single-arm bench row, `Isometric Squat to Failure` is a wall sit. Name-based matching would have
   produced wrong assignments in both directions.
