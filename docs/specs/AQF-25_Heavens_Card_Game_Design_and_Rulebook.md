# AQUA ZERO HEAVENS: The Shen Engine

Document ID: AQF-25, Version 2.0

This version replaces the v1.0 rulebook in full. It merges the Shen card
challenge system (health bars, timed turns, Attack, Defence and Energy stances,
four card tiers, fusion and the token economy) with the speed and simplicity of
the best one on one card fighters, and it grounds every technique card in real
martial arts drawn from the source library: the traditional Muay Thai weapon
families (Cherng Mad, Cherng Sok, Cherng Khao, Cherng Thao and the Mae Mai
master tricks), Kukkiwon Taekwondo kick terminology (the chagi system), Gracie
method Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, catch wrestling, judo, sambo and sumo.

The design target is stated once and everything serves it: **a full fight in
under fifteen minutes, learnable in one game, with real martial arts on every
card.**

# PART 1: STRATEGY, KEPT SHORT

The full commercial analysis lives in v1 and still stands. The load bearing
points:

1. **Build the character brand first, the collectible second.** The failure
   case is a token economy with no game underneath it. The success case is an
   IP with a genuinely fun game, where the collectible is a claim on characters
   people already love.
2. **The wellness firewall stands.** Shen tokens, XP purchases and the NFT
   market live entirely inside the game. Nothing tradeable is ever earned by
   logging food, logging weight or completing workouts in AquaZeroFit. The
   bridge between app and game is identity (owning a fighter unlocks their
   coach persona), never money.
3. **The legal flags stand.** A purchasable token, publicity adjacent fighter
   names, and generated card art each need counsel before any sale. Flagged in
   v1 Appendix B, unresolved, and none of it blocks playtesting.

# PART 2: THE GAME IN ONE PAGE

Two fighters. **100 health each.** Health bars on the table or on screen.

Each turn, both players have **12 seconds** to secretly commit cards face down,
then both reveal at once. You commit to one of three stances:

- **ATTACK**: up to 3 attack cards as a combo. Damage is printed on every card.
- **DEFENCE**: up to 3 defence cards. Block values are printed on every card.
- **ENERGY**: up to 2 energy cards. Draw, recover health, or power up.

The triangle that decides exchanges:

- **DEFENCE beats ATTACK**: blocks soak the damage, a perfect block counters.
- **ATTACK beats ENERGY**: you caught them loading, bonus damage.
- **ENERGY beats DEFENCE**: they guarded nothing while you built resources.

A fight is **three rounds of five exchanges**, 180 fight seconds. Between
rounds you play Corner cards, the help layer. Big hits inflict Condition
cards, the injury layer. Fights end by **knockout** (0 health), **submission**
(a finisher landed on a wounded opponent), or **decision** (more health after
round three).

Position: the fight is either **STANDING** or on the **GROUND**. Takedowns put
it down, stand ups bring it back. Submissions only work where they really
work.

That is the whole game. Everything else is detail.

# PART 3: THE COMPLETE RULEBOOK

## 3.1 Components

|---|---|---|

| Special Technique cards | 3 | Your fighter's unique moves, one copy each |

| Health tracker | 1 | A 100 point dial, bar or d100 |

| Component | Quantity | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Position marker | 1 | STANDING or GROUND |
| Condition deck | 26 | Injuries and surges, drawn on heavy hits |
| Corner deck | 24 | Between round help cards |
| Arena card | 1 | The venue, changes the bout |
| 12 second timer | 1 | Sand timer in paper, automatic in digital |

## 3.2 Formats

**Quickstart (paper, first game).** Each fighter has a fixed 45 card deck: 24
attack, 12 defence, 6 energy, plus their 3 specials shuffled in. Two
Quickstart decks and the shared decks fit in one small box. Akin's full
Quickstart list is Appendix A.

**Constructed (digital and tournament).** Minimum deck size **135 cards**
including your character's 3 specials. Maximum 3 copies of any named card,
specials limited to 1 copy each. At least 30 defence cards and at least 15
energy cards, so no deck can be all offence.

**The one duplication rule, all formats:** you may never play two cards with
the same name in the same turn, whatever their tier.

## 3.3 Setup

1. Choose fighters. Reveal one Arena card and apply it for the whole bout.
2. Set both health trackers to the fighter's printed health (100 at Standard
   tier).
3. Set the Position marker to STANDING.
4. Shuffle. Draw an opening hand of **6**.
5. The lighter fighter is the challenger and wins all ties of timing.

## 3.4 The turn, in full

Every turn is the same four beats. A practised pair finishes one in well under
thirty seconds.

**Beat 1, The Clock.** Start the 12 second timer. Both players place cards
face down, committing to one stance. You may place fewer than the maximum. If
the timer runs out before you have placed anything, you have **skipped**: an
undefended turn (see 3.6).

**Beat 2, Reveal.** Both players turn their cards over simultaneously.

**Beat 3, Resolve.** Use the stance table in 3.5. Damage comes off health
immediately. Health can never go below 0 or above the printed maximum.

**Beat 4, Refill.** Discard everything you played. Draw back up to 6. An empty
deck reshuffles its own discard pile.

After five turns the round ends: go to the Corner (3.9).

## 3.5 The stance table

| You | Opponent | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| ATTACK | ATTACK | A slugfest. Both combos land in full. Both players take the other's total. |
| ATTACK | DEFENCE | Your damage total minus their block total gets through. If block covers everything, they have made a **Perfect Parry**, see below. |
| ATTACK | ENERGY | You caught them loading. Your combo lands with **+4 bonus damage**. Their energy cards still resolve afterwards if they are still conscious. |
| ENERGY | DEFENCE | Your energy cards resolve in full. They guarded nothing, but composure is worth something: they recover 2 health. |
| ENERGY | ENERGY | Both resolve. Both players also draw 1 extra card. |
| DEFENCE | DEFENCE | A staring match. Nothing happens. The crowd boos. Both draw 1. |

**Perfect Parry.** If you played defence and your block total covered the
entire incoming damage, you immediately play one attack card from your hand as
a free counter, and it deals **double damage**. This is the Shen rule that
rewards committing to defence, and it is why a wall of blocks is never
passive.

**Clash of Maximums.** If one player revealed **3 attack cards** and the other
revealed **3 defence cards** in the same turn, do not calculate. **Flip a
coin.** If the attacker wins the flip, the full combo lands at **double
damage**. If the defender wins, it is an automatic Perfect Parry and their
counter deals double damage as above. Maximum commitment against maximum
commitment is a moment of pure nerve, and the game honours it with drama
rather than arithmetic.

**Combo bonus.** A 3 card attack combo that lands (any damage gets through)
adds **+3 flow damage**. Combos are the reason to hold cards back.

**Specials in combos.** At most **one** Special Technique card per turn. It
counts toward your 3 attack cards unless its text says otherwise.

## 3.6 Skipping and stalling

A player who commits nothing, or whose timer expires empty, takes the
opponent's revealed cards **unopposed with +4 bonus damage**, exactly as if
caught on ENERGY with nothing to show for it. There is no safe turn in a cage.

## 3.7 Position: STANDING and GROUND

- The marker starts on STANDING every round.
- Cards tagged **TAKEDOWN** move it to GROUND when they land (deal any damage
  or resolve unblocked).
- Cards tagged **STAND UP** move it back to STANDING.
- Cards tagged **GROUND ONLY** cannot be committed while STANDING, and cards
  tagged **STANDING ONLY** cannot be committed on the GROUND. Check when you
  commit, not after.
- **Home Turf:** every character card names STANDING or GROUND. While the
  marker is on your home turf, every attack card you land deals **+1**, and
  your POWER bonus (3.8) applies twice.

## 3.8 Damage, health and injuries

- All fighters have **100 health** at Standard tier (tier bonuses in Part 5).
- Character cards carry a **POWER** value from their weight class, added once
  to each turn's total when at least one attack card lands:

| Weight class | POWER | Hand size |
|---|---|---|
| Lightweight and Welterweight | +0 | 7 |
| Middleweight | +1 | 6 |
| Light Heavyweight | +2 | 6 |
| Heavyweight | +3 | 5 |

- **Conditions.** Any time a fighter takes **18 or more damage in one turn**,
  they draw one Condition card and resolve it immediately. Conditions are the
  fight leaving marks (4.4).
- **Finishers.** Submission cards state a health threshold, for example
  "Finisher 25". If the opponent's health is at or below that number when the
  submission lands, **the bout ends immediately by submission**. Above the
  threshold the card just deals its printed damage. Position rules apply.

## 3.9 The Corner phase

After turns five and ten (the ends of rounds one and two):

1. **Score the round.** More damage dealt that round takes the 10 to 9 marker.
2. **Corner cards.** Each player draws 2 from the Corner deck, plays 1,
   discards the other. Your fighter's own Signature Corner card can be played
   instead, once per bout.
3. **Treat.** Resolve any Condition that says it is treated in the corner.
4. Reset the Position marker to STANDING, refill hands to full, and begin the
   next round. Health does **not** reset between rounds. A fight remembers.

## 3.10 Winning

- **Knockout:** opponent at 0 health, at any moment. Immediate.
- **Submission:** a finisher landed at or below its threshold. Immediate.
- **Decision:** after round three, more remaining health wins. Equal health
  goes to round markers, then to total damage dealt, then it is a **draw**,
  which is printed on the scorecard and not broken by a coin.
- **Simultaneous KO** (a slugfest that empties both bars) is a double
  knockout: a draw, and the single best story a table can tell.

## 3.11 Timing rules that end arguments

- The 12 seconds run from the call of "clock". Digital enforces it exactly;
  paper uses a sand timer and honour.
- Where two effects resolve at once, the player who took more damage this turn
  chooses the order.
- Hands are private. Health, Position, Conditions in play and discard piles
  are public.
- A card revealed in an illegal stance mix (an attack card inside a defence
  commitment, say) is discarded without effect. The rest of the commitment
  stands.

## 3.12 Bracket mode, 3 to 8 players

Single elimination. Health carries scars: fighters start each new bout at
**maximum minus 10 per bout already fought**. Conditions marked **Lingering**
persist between bouts. The bracket winner faces **Ogun Celsus, the
Sovereign**, piloted by the eliminated player with the most total damage
across the tournament. Ogun is meant to be unfair. Beating him should be a
story people tell for years.

# PART 5: CHARACTER CARDS, TIERS AND THE COLLECTION

## 5.1 The four tiers

Every fighter exists in four printings. The frame is the rarity, exactly as
specified in the Shen design notes:

| Tier | Frame | Character bonus |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | White and bronze | Baseline: 100 health, printed POWER |
| **Elite** | Black and silver | 110 health, POWER +1 |
| **Grandmaster** | Black, red and gold | 120 health, POWER +1, one chosen Special may be played twice per bout |
| **God Tier** | Crystal and platinum | 130 health, POWER +2, all Specials deal +2, unique aura art |

Tier bonuses apply in casual play, the campaign and the open ladder.
**Sanctioned ranked play normalises every character to Standard statistics**:
the frame still shines and the collection still matters, but the podium is
earned by play. That one sentence is what keeps a competitive scene alive long
enough for the collection to be worth anything.

## 5.2 Levelling and fusion

- Winning grants **XP** to the character used. Levels unlock cosmetics and
  campaign content.
- **Fusion:** three copies of the same fighter at the same tier fuse into one
  copy at the next tier. Standard x3 becomes Elite, Elite x3 becomes
  Grandmaster, Grandmaster x3 becomes God Tier. Fusion burns the inputs, which
  gives the upper tiers scarcity without a lottery.

## 5.3 Shen tokens

The in game currency, earned by winning, spendable on XP, cosmetics, deck
slots, marketplace fees and characters. One warning repeated from v1 because
it is the one that ends companies: **if tokens are emitted for play faster
than the game burns them, the economy is a pyramid with better art.** Emission
capped by sinks from day one, and a purchasable token needs securities counsel
before sale in Australia and the United States.

## 5.4 What ownership gets you

1. The card is playable in the digital client.
2. Owning a fighter unlocks that fighter as your AquaZeroFit coach persona,
   permanently. The entitlement system already exists in the app.
3. A commercial licence to the artwork you own.
4. A printed physical copy of any card you hold.

What it never gets you: an advantage in sanctioned ranked play, yield, or
anything connected to wellness app activity.

# PART 6: THE ROSTER AT FULL SIZE

Unchanged in structure from v1: **33 fighters**, one Sovereign outside the
bracket and 32 across four Quadrants of eight, released a Quadrant at a time.
Before art, every new fighter needs: a cleared name, weight class, home turf,
**exactly 3 Special Techniques drawn from real martial arts**, a Signature
Corner card in their voice, a reason they entered, and a relationship to an
existing fighter. The nine written fighters with their new specials are in
AQF-26 v2.0.

Balance targets across 32: roughly half STANDING and half GROUND home turf,
no more than a third of the roster leaning submission finishes, every weight
class present in every Quadrant.

# APPENDIX B: DESIGN LINEAGE

clock, the three stances, the coin flip on maximum against maximum, the skip
penalty, 100 health, three rounds inside 180 fight seconds, the four frame

stance triangle's interaction pattern is the proven loop of the best one on
one card fighters. Position, finishers, Conditions, Corners and Arenas

the source library: the Cherng weapon families and Mae Mai of traditional Muay
