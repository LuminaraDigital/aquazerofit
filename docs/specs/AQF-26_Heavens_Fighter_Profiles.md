# AQUA ZERO HEAVENS: Fighter Profiles

Document ID: AQF-26, Version 2.0

Rebuilt in full for the Shen Engine. Every fighter now carries **exactly three
Special Technique cards**, each a real technique from a real martial art, named
in the art's own language, with damage printed for play. Character statistics
follow AQF-25 v2.0: **100 health at Standard tier**, a POWER bonus from weight
class, a Home Turf of STANDING or GROUND, and the four tier printings
(Standard, Elite, Grandmaster, God Tier).

Each profile has six blocks: **Tale of the Tape** (the card face), **The
Fighter** (who they are), **Style** (how they fight), **Special Techniques**
(the three unique cards, print ready), **Corner** (their once per bout
signature), and **Art Direction**.

# TIER ONE: THE CELSUS BLOODLINE

Father figure and son. The storm and the sky. Mechanically deliberate
opposites: one wins by dragging the fight to his world, the other has no world
he is not already home in.

## 01. AKIN CELSUS, "DIVINUS"

### Tale of the Tape

| | |
|---|---|
| Full name | Akin Nguyen Celsus |
| Ring name | Divinus |
| Born | Melbourne, Australia, age 18 |
| Heritage | African American father, Vietnamese mother, raised across the USA and Singapore |
| Height and Reach | 6 ft 4 in, 80 in reach |
| Weight class | Middleweight, 185 lb |
| Disciplines | Muay Thai, Lethwei, Taekwondo, Brazilian Jiu Jitsu in progress, MMA |
| **Health** | **100** (110 Elite, 120 Grandmaster, 130 God Tier) |
| **Power** | **+1** (Middleweight) |
| **Hand size** | 6 |
| **Home Turf** | **STANDING** |
| Finish lean | Knockout |
| Theme colour | Cyan white on black |
| Quadrant | I, The Proving Ground |
| Card number | AZH 001, printings in all four tiers plus the Muay Thai Gear Variant |

### The Fighter

Akin is two people sharing a body, and the tournament is where they finally
have to agree. On the surface he is disarmingly ordinary: an eighteen year old
information technology student who finished school at sixteen, quick to grin,
quicker to help. Underneath is a young man who decided that the only way to
never lose anyone again was to become strong enough that loss could not reach
him.

He is in love with fighting itself. He lights up in close, seeks out people
who can hurt him the way others seek comfort, and treats a beating as a gift
because it shows him exactly where he is weak. He grins with a split lip. He
says "again" when he should be unconscious. Combat is the only place the grief
goes quiet, which he knows and has never said aloud.

Wrapped around the hunger is a blade of discipline: never break someone who
has yielded, never fight someone who cannot defend themselves, and the code is
enforced hardest on himself. The story the series keeps circling is what
happens when the code and the hunger want different things. His arc is not
getting stronger. It is learning that letting people close is not handing fate
a hostage, and he learns it in a coaching gym, because coaching is the first
strength anyone ever showed him that is given away.

**Core traits:** hungry, warm, principled, self punishing, brilliant, haunted.
**Flaw:** he confuses intimacy with liability.

### Style

A striker's striker with a stitched together base: Taekwondo footwork and
spinning kicks, Muay Thai's weapons and rhythm, Lethwei's bare knuckle
honesty, and a ground game being built from nothing, which is his real
weakness and should stay that way. On the card table he is the teaching
fighter: he wants the marker on STANDING, his specials reward staying there,
and a new player learns the whole game by playing him.

### Special Techniques

Three cards, one copy each, the user defined signature set: one Muay Thai, one
Taekwondo, one Brazilian Jiu Jitsu.

| Card | Art | Verb | Damage | Tags | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jorakhe Fad Hang, the Crocodile Sweeps Its Tail** | Muay Thai | Attack | **18** | STANDING ONLY | The spinning heel kick of the old Mae Mai. High risk: if this combo is fully blocked, take 5 yourself. On 18+ turn damage the Condition drawn is Rocked. |
| **Dolgae Chagi, Tornado Kick** | Taekwondo | Attack | **15** | STANDING ONLY | The 360 degree turning roundhouse. +5 when played as the 3rd card of a combo, the spin feeds on momentum. |
| **Gogoplata** | Brazilian Jiu Jitsu | Attack | **8** | GROUND ONLY, **Finisher 25** | The shin across the throat, from the rubber guard. Cannot be answered by a Perfect Parry. If it does not finish, the opponent discards 1 card. |

### Corner: "Again, Let Us Go Again"

> "You are still here. That is the whole fight, showing up when it stops being
> exciting. Hands up. Again."

Recover 10 health and draw 2. If you lost the round just scored, recover 20
instead. Strongest when he is behind, which is the entire character.

### Art Direction

**Standard:** full body, fighting stance, cyan and white on black. Young,
long limbed, visibly a teenager in a way nobody else on the roster is. The
grin matters and illustrators will keep dropping it. Do not let them.

**Variant, Muay Thai Gear (produced, AZH 001 V):** red and black AZX trunks,
red MMA gloves, red ankle wraps, guard high. The face is set instead of
grinning: the other half of the character, and the launch Variant.

**Expression sheet:** neutral, grinning, and The Quiet, the same face with
everything switched off.

## 02. OGUN CELSUS, "THE DAEMON KING"

### Tale of the Tape

| | |
|---|---|
| Full name | Baba Ogun Celsus |
| Ring name | The Daemon King |
| Born | Washington D.C., USA, 22 July, age 37 |
| Height and Reach | 6 ft 6 in, 84 in reach |
| Weight class | Light Heavyweight, 205 lb |
| Disciplines | MMA, Taekwondo, Muay Thai. The complete fighter. |
| **Health** | **120 as the Sovereign** (he does not follow the table, that is the point) |
| **Power** | **+2**, applied twice everywhere: every position is his Home Turf |
| **Hand size** | 6 |
| **Home Turf** | **Any** |
| Finish lean | Knockout |
| Theme colour | Obsidian black, blood crimson |
| Quadrant | Sovereign, outside the bracket, not legal in ranked play |

### The Fighter

Ogun does not enter a room; the room reorganises itself around him. He
founded the conglomerate, brokered champions from six sports into one
unsanctioned arena, and built the Heavens Tournament for a reason he has
never hidden: he was bored of a world that could not challenge him.

His defining quality is serene certainty. Never rushed, never surprised,
almost never impressed. Cruelty and generosity are the same instrument, and
he will fund an orphanage and end a career in the same afternoon without
noticing a contradiction. He smiles most warmly right before doing something
terrible, and the smile is real. The crack in the armour: he genuinely enjoys
strength wherever he finds it, and a worthy opponent can make him delighted.
A delighted Ogun is more dangerous than an angry one.

He does not hate Akin; hatred would be a respect he has not decided to grant.
He is curious the way a god is curious about a promising mortal, and he built
a global spectacle partly as a crucible to find out. Every kindness is a
test, and every test, in its own monstrous way, is love.

**Core traits:** sovereign, serene, magnetic, amoral, insatiable.
**Flaw:** he cannot imagine an equal, so he keeps building larger arenas
trying to manufacture one. It is loneliness, and he would never name it.

### Style

The complete fighter. On the table he ignores the game's central law: every
position is his Home Turf, so the war over the marker that defines every
other bout simply does not apply to him. He is the final boss, he is meant to
feel unfair, and he is not legal in ranked play.

### Special Techniques

| Card | Art | Verb | Damage | Tags | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Verdict** | Boxing | Attack | **20** | | One casually thrown strike. Playable only in round three, or while the opponent is at 35 health or below. |
| **Twio Dwi Chagi, Jumping Back Kick** | Taekwondo | Attack | **16** | STANDING ONLY | Ignores 4 points of block. Delivered like weather. |
| **Mata Leao, the Lion Killer** | BJJ / MMA | Attack | **9** | **Finisher 30**, any position | The rear naked choke, and he does not need the ground to find your neck. |

### Corner: "Discipline Is The Only Throne"

> "You came back. Good. Discipline is the only throne that cannot be
> inherited. You build it, one session at a time."

Recover 8 health per round already fought. He gets stronger as the night gets
longer, which is correct for a final boss and wrong for anyone else.

### Art Direction

**Standard:** black and crimson, the long coat, enormous but not bulky, the
face amused rather than angry, which is harder to draw and much more
frightening.

**Variant, Gold Dobok (produced, AZH 000 V):** gold and black taekwondo dobok,
black belt, barefoot, arms loose. The practitioner instead of the magnate,
and the flagship Variant of the launch. The stillness is the whole image; do
not let an illustrator add a stance.

**Expression sheet:** neutral, the warm smile, and Daemon Face, drawn as the
back rather than the face.

## 03. ANDERSON COUTURE, "THE GIANT"

### Tale of the Tape

| | |
|---|---|
| Ring name | The Giant |
| Born | Ohio, USA, age 37 |
| Height and Reach | 6 ft 9 in, 81 in reach |
| Weight class | Heavyweight, 255 lb |
| Disciplines | Collegiate wrestling, boxing |

| **Hand size** | 5 |

| Theme colour | Iron grey, warning red |
| Quadrant | II, The Old Guard |

### The Fighter

Anderson learned long ago that the world flinches when he walks in, and made
a decision about it: be gentle everywhere except the ring. Outside the cage
he is soft spoken and almost courtly, a wrestler and boxer who turned
professional late and never lost the humility of a man who earned every
pound. He advises the young fighters, and he checks on the man he just
knocked out, every time, unasked.

Inside the cage the gentleness inverts into something suffocating. He does
not rush. He walks you down, absorbs what you have, and leans his whole
history on you until something decides it has had enough. He fights because
it is honest work, not because it is who he is, which makes him the perfect
counterweight to the Celsus obsession. He respects Akin's hunger and quietly
worries about it.

**Core traits:** patient, humble, protective, immovable.
**Flaw:** patience tips into passivity; faster men take what he waits for.

### Style

Walk down pressure and heavyweight arithmetic: hand size 5 and POWER +3 means
every card he plays must count, and does. His specials convert absorbed
punishment into position.

### Special Techniques

| Card | Art | Verb | Damage | Tags | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ohio Overhand** | Boxing | Attack | **14** | | +4 if the opponent revealed ATTACK last turn. He eats one to land one, and his lands heavier. |
| **German Suplex** | Wrestling | Attack | **12** | TAKEDOWN, STANDING ONLY | Must be your only attack card this turn. The arena floor is a weapon. |
| **Cradle Ride** | Wrestling | Attack | **6** | GROUND ONLY | The opponent cannot play ENERGY as their stance next turn. Church is in session. |

### Corner: "Slow Is Smooth"

> "Slow is smooth, smooth is strong. We add weight when the movement earns
> it, and not one round before."

Your block totals gain +3 for the whole next round.

### Art Direction

Iron grey and warning red. Communicate the size by how small everything
around him is, not by exaggerated muscle. The face must read as kind. That
contrast is the character.

### The Fighter

Jacare carries himself like royalty because in the neighbourhood that raised
him he genuinely is: the local boy who fought his way out and sends every
purse home. The title is half swagger, half obligation. He is flamboyant,
fast talking, theatrical, and he means every second of it.

Under the swagger sits something much colder. He will clown for the cameras
for two minutes and then finish you in fourteen seconds with the smile still
on. He is exactly the ground game master Akin lacks, their rivalry is the one
most likely to become the series' warmest friendship, and he teases Akin
without mercy while wanting him to be better.

**Core traits:** flamboyant, generous, sharp, secretly disciplined.
**Flaw:** vanity. He plays to the crowd one beat too long and gets caught.

### Style

A scrambler. Every position is an attack, every exchange sets up the next
one, and he will happily lose a position to reach a better one. The player
who loves Jacare is the player who loves being two moves ahead.

### The Fighter

King is the spiritual centre of the striking arts in this world, raised in a
temple gym with the ritual still in his bones. He bows to the ring, to his
opponent, and to the memory of every teacher before he throws a single knee.
Where Akin found fighting through grief, King found it through reverence, and
that contrast is why he exists: King is what Akin might have become in a
gentler life.

Quiet, observant, and under the calm a temple lion. In the unsanctioned scene
back home his reputation is near mythical, and it is implied he crossed paths
with Akin during the lost year, which makes their eventual meeting carry a
loaded, unspoken intimacy neither will explain.

**Core traits:** devout, disciplined, serene, ferocious when it counts.
**Flaw:** reverence makes him readable; he honours the form even when
breaking it would win.

### Style

Eight limbs as one instrument, arriving on a rhythm rather than from a
favourite weapon. Hand size 7 makes him the tempo fighter: more options every
turn, and specials that convert rhythm into damage.

### The Fighter

A champion before most of the bracket was born, back to prove one
proposition: technique outlives youth. Theatrical dignity, iron
fundamentals, and a psychological game refined enough to win fights in the
opponent's head before the bell. They call him The Hunter because he studies
prey; he knows your habits better than you do and lets you show him three
before he uses one.

Outside the ring he is warm and grandfatherly, a keeper of old methods who
disapproves gently of the tournament's brutality while advancing through it.
For Akin he is the long game made flesh: proof a fighter can grow wiser
rather than only harder.

**Core traits:** cunning, dignified, patient, theatrical, wise.
**Flaw:** the body is fifty. He must finish before the fight becomes a
marathon.

### Style

He does not attack; he invites, and punishes what arrives. The roster's
counter fighter, the only one whose specials reward being attacked, and by a
distance the most satisfying pilot against an aggressor.

### The Fighter

Control made into a person. Raised in the mountains inside a grappling
tradition with no interest in spectacle, his philosophy is smothering
inevitability: take the position, take the next one, remove options until
none remain. Stoic almost to silence, deeply serious, carrying a region's
expectations on shoulders built for exactly that.

Behind the wall, a fierce loyalty: his people, his village, his father's
name, in that order. He regards the flashy fighters with quiet disdain and
Akin's joy in battle as a luxury nobody ever offered him. Chaos against
control, and neither has met the other's equal.

**Core traits:** stoic, relentless, disciplined, loyal.
**Flaw:** rigidity. Genuine unpredictability makes him hesitate, and his
style cannot absorb hesitation.

### Style

Chain grappling that never lets you stand back up. The grind is the point:
his specials strip options rather than chase highlights, and he is
deliberately the most frustrating fighter in the game to sit across from.

the weapon.

### The Fighter

The fighter nobody can prepare for, because he does not appear to know what

air of a magician turning over a card he never touched. Behind the
