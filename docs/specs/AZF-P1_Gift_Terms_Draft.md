# Gift a Round: terms draft for counsel review

Status: **draft for review, not published.** Nothing here is on `/terms`. Gifting
(`FC_GIFTING_ENABLED`) must stay off until counsel has approved wording and it is
live on `/terms` under a `#gifts` heading, which is where the gift sheet links.

This draft describes what the software does today, so the terms can promise exactly
that and no more. The open questions at the end are for counsel and the accountant.

## What the software does

- **What a gift is.** Seven days of AquaZeroFit Premium for one other person. There
  are no other sizes, bundles, tiers or random rewards.
- **Who can give and receive.** The gifter and the recipient are both adults (18 or
  over) and active members of the same Fight Camp squad this season. The recipient
  does not have Premium when the gift is bought. A gift cannot be sent to someone
  outside the gifter's squad, to the gifter, or anonymously.
- **Where.** Inside the AquaZeroFit Telegram Mini App only, paid with Telegram Stars.
  It is not offered on the website.
- **Price.** A fixed number of Stars, shown on the gift sheet before anything is paid,
  and the same for everyone. The price can change between gifts but never during one.
- **When it starts.** The seven days start when payment succeeds. The recipient sees
  "<Name> gifted you a week of Premium" in the app.
- **Refunds.** Requested through `/paysupport` in the AquaZeroFit bot. A refund
  returns the Stars to the person who paid and ends the recipient's gifted days that
  are left. Days already used are not taken back from the recipient.
- **Records.** A record of each gift (amount, status, dates) is kept for tax and
  refund handling. If either person deletes their account, their identity is removed
  from that record and the record itself stays.

## Draft wording

> **Gifts.** In the AquaZeroFit Telegram Mini App you can buy another adult member of
> your Fight Camp squad seven days of Premium, paid in Telegram Stars. The price is
> shown before you pay. The seven days start when your payment succeeds. You can only
> gift someone who does not already have Premium. A gift has no cash value and cannot
> be exchanged or transferred. To ask for a refund, message the AquaZeroFit bot with
> /paysupport. If a gift is refunded, the Stars go back to you and any of the seven
> days the recipient has not yet used end. Telegram's own terms for Stars also apply.
